## Kernel dependency audit

[`AxiomAudit.lean`](https://huggingface.co/spaces/DineshAI/kovefbSXbQ/blob/main/verification/Formalization/AxiomAudit.lean)
runs `#print axioms` on every public theorem. No project axiom is declared.
Seven theorems require no axiom. The remaining four use only Lean's trusted
logical foundations:

```text
claim3_safe_verification:
  Classical.choice, Quot.sound
claim4_zero_predicate_commutes:
  Quot.sound
claim5_next_observation_prediction:
  Quot.sound
claim5_model_irrelevance_iff_homomorphism:
  propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound
claim5_bisimulation_quotient_iff_postfixed:
  Quot.sound
```

`Classical.choice` is needed to choose representatives from arbitrary
surjective fibers; `Quot.sound` implements quotient equality; `propext`
supports the logical equivalence in Proposition 4.2. These are trusted Lean
kernel principles, not paper-specific assumptions inserted as axioms.

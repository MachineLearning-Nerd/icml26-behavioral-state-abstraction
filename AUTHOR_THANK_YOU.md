# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yivan Zhang, Ziyan Luo, and Manuel Baltieri for developing
*Compositional Behavioral Semantics for State Abstraction in Reinforcement
Learning* and making the paper and source materials available. The paper’s
explicit definitions, assumptions, and proposition structure made it possible
to audit both the finite mechanics and the stronger Lean proof routes.

This repository is an independent reproduction and formalization audit. It
records scoped evidence and limitations and does not imply review, endorsement,
or approval by the authors.
